# Content rating questionnaire (IARC) — Play Console › Policy › App content › Content rating

Answer conservatively: a rating that's too strict costs little, an inaccurate one can get the app
pulled. Expected outcome: **PEGI 7 / ESRB Everyone 10+ / USK 6 or 12**, mainly for fantasy
violence in card art and for users being able to talk to each other. The final rating is whatever
IARC returns; don't argue it down.

## Category

**"All other app types"** (it is not a game, a social network first, a news app or a store). If the
questionnaire offers "Reference, News or Educational" vs "Entertainment/other", choose the latter.

## Moderation note for the owner

Because users can message each other and upload pictures, Play's User Generated Content policy
applies: the app must have (it does) a way to report and block users, and the owner must act on
reports. Reports land in `public.reports` (status `new`); check them regularly, and say so in the
UGC declaration if Play asks.
