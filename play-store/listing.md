# Store listing (Play Console › Grow users › Store presence › Main store listing)

Plain British English. Character counts are checked by `play-store/check-lengths.py`
(`python3 play-store/check-lengths.py`); Play counts characters, not bytes.

## App name (30 max)

```
Manabind: MTG Companion
```

Alternatives, if the owner prefers: `Manabind – Magic card manager`, `Manabind: Decks, Binders, Life`.

Note on the name: "MTG" and "Magic: The Gathering" are Wizards of the Coast trademarks. Using "MTG"
descriptively is common on Play, but Play's intellectual-property policy lets a rights holder
complain. The full description ends with Wizards' Fan Content Policy wording, which the website
should carry too. If the owner would rather not take the risk, use a name without "MTG".

## Short description (80 max)

```
Scan and sort your Magic cards, build decks, track games and trade with friends.
```

## Full description (4000 max)

```
Manabind keeps your Magic: The Gathering collection, decks and games in one place, on your phone and on the web at manabind.com. It's free, with no ads.

SCAN YOUR CARDS
Point the camera at a card and it's recognised: no shutter button, just keep going through the pile. Recognition runs on your phone. Mark scans as foil, send them to a binder or a deck, and undo anything.

KNOW WHERE EVERY CARD IS
• Make places for your real cards: shelves, boxes, binders and deck boxes, one inside another.
• Put cards away with the scanner and each one tells you where to file it.
• Sort a new pile into rares, spares, cards your decks want and bulk.
• Flip through a binder page by page, and slot new cards in order, step by step.
• Check a box by scanning it: see what's missing and what doesn't belong.
• Print box labels, then scan a label to open the box.

YOUR COLLECTION
• Binders and a wishlist, with condition, language and foil for every copy.
• Set completion, and missing cards one tap from the wishlist.
• Prices in your currency, a card's price history, and alerts when a wishlist card drops or one you own rises to your price.
• Value by place, a breakdown by set, colour and rarity, and your most valuable cards.
• Advanced filters: colour and commander identity, mana value, legality, keywords, price, artist and more. Save the ones you use.
• Spread thin: the cards your decks need more copies of than you own.
• Lend cards to friends and remember who has what.
• Import and export lists and spreadsheets.

BUILD DECKS
• Commander, Brawl, 60-card formats and draft or sealed.
• Pick a commander from every legal one, with partners and backgrounds.
• Cards are checked before they go in: banned, colour identity, copy limits.
• Suggestions, combos, a mana curve and stats, sideboards, categories and a primer for each deck.
• Playtest with mulligans, turns and your deck's tokens.
• Build this deck: a pull list that walks you round your boxes and binders. Take apart puts every card back.
• Export for Arena and MTGO, or compare with another deck.

PLAY
• A life counter for 1 to 10 players, with commander damage, poison, energy, dungeons, the Ring and more.
• Other phones can join the table as remotes and buzz on their turn.
• A game clock, turn timer, life chart and recap after every game.
• Game night: fair pods by power. Events: run a small Swiss or Commander tournament.
• Your playgroup's record, streaks and nemesis, and a shared game log for your pod.

FRIENDS (OPTIONAL)
• Add friends, share decks and binders, and see who has the card you need.
• Propose trades, message friends, and get a notification when someone answers.
• Block and report people.

AN ACCOUNT IS OPTIONAL
Everything above works without signing in, and offline. Sign in with an email address to sync your library between phones and manabind.com, and to use the friends features. You can delete your account at any time from Settings.

Card data and images come from Scryfall. Combos come from Commander Spellbook, deck suggestions from EDHREC and precon lists from MTGJSON.

Manabind is unofficial Fan Content permitted under the Fan Content Policy. Not approved or endorsed by Wizards. Portions of the materials used are property of Wizards of the Coast. ©Wizards of the Coast LLC.
```

## Other listing fields

- **App type / category:** App (not Game) › **Entertainment**. Play has no "card game companion"
  category; Entertainment fits a hobby tool, and listing under Games would suggest Manabind is
  itself a game. Lifestyle is the alternative.
- **Tags:** collection, card games, trading cards.
- **Contact email:** required, shown publicly. The owner chooses one (it isn't in the code).
