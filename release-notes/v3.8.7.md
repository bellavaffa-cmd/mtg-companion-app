## Storage with rules, binders that include your deck cards, and a scanner that sorts

### New
- **Storage rules.** Tell each box or binder what it holds (any filter: "blue cards", "rares over $5", "lands"), how it's split into sections (colour, card type, set, rarity, value…) and how cards are sorted inside (A–Z, number, mana value, price). Put away and "Let the app choose" send every card to the right place and section, a box shows cards that don't belong with a Move button, and Sort a pile has a new "Into my storage" recipe. When two places could hold a card, you choose which comes first in Storage › Rule order.
- **Search and filter inside storage.** Every box and binder has the same search and filters as Cards, with "14 of 312 cards · $118" at the top, and the Storage tab finds matching cards across all your places. Walk this list turns the results into a pull list in the order the cards sit.
- **One scanner, four jobs.** Switch between Add cards, Sort a pile, Put away and Check at the top of the scanner. Sort a pile starts from your last recipe.
- **Binders include your deck cards.** A card in a deck can still be listed in a binder — it shows "In Talrand deck", and its place is the deck. Use "Add to binder…" on a deck card or "Put all cards in a binder…" on a deck. "Unsorted" is now called **No binder**, and lists your deck cards that aren't in a binder yet. Your collection's count and value don't change.
- **Why a deck isn't legal.** The deck's label says why ("Not legal · 3 banned", "· 98/100 cards", "· no commander"); tap it for every problem and quick fixes.
- **Card tags for every card.** All your cards are tagged in the background, including tokens and proxies, with new Land, Fixing and Vanilla tags, an "Untagged" filter, and Settings › Data and speed › Card tags.

### Fixes
- Switching tabs in a deck could crash the app.
- The Wishlist tab now scrolls like the other tabs.

### Which file to download

- **app-release-arm64-v8a.apk**: almost every phone from the last several years.
- **app-universal-release.apk**: works on any phone. Use it if the one above won't install.
