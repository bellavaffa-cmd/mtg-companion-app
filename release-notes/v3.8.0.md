## Friends, front and centre

Everything from tester builds 32 to 35, in one release.

### A new Friends tab
- The bottom bar is now **Home · Search · Play | Scan | Decks · Collection · Friends**, with a badge on Friends for unread chats, trades waiting for you and friend requests.
- **People:** your next game night, your pods (with your place in the league) and a line on each friend — what they want from you, a card they've borrowed, a shelf you share.
- **Chats:** one list for direct messages and a **group chat for each pod**, with league results posted in it.
- **Trades:** your turn, waiting on them and done, each with a fairness bar, plus "What friends want from you" across all your binders.
- **Activity:** a friend's new trade card that's on your wishlist ("Ask Priya for it"), new decks, league news and friends' selling lists. Choose what you share in Settings › Privacy.
- **Play** is now just the table: start a game, the next game night, game night, playgroup, events and Pack your bag.

### Game nights and leagues
- **Game night invites:** pick a day and place, invite your pod, and answer Going, Maybe or Can't. Pack your bag and trade matches fill in from who's coming, with a reminder the day before and Add to calendar.
- **League mode:** seasons for your pod with simple scoring, a league table, a champion and past seasons.
- **Trade matches tonight:** for each friend at the game night, the cards they want that you have spare, and where they are.

### Decks
- **Comments on shared decks:** friends can comment on the deck or one card in it, and you can reply.
- **Print proxies:** the cards a deck is missing, nine to a page at real card size.
- **Hand odds:** in the playtest, how often you'll have your lands and a two-drop on time, from 10,000 hands.

### Collecting and trading
- **Trade fairness:** both sides' totals and the difference, with suggestions to even it out.
- **Collection value over time:** a chart of your collection's value, with the biggest risers and fallers.
- **New sets:** upcoming and new sets, the cards in them that fit your Commander decks, and reprints of wishlist cards. Follow a set for a release-day notification.

### Scanning
- **Scan sounds by rarity:** a different sound for common, uncommon, rare and mythic cards, a sparkle for foils, and an optional sting for cards worth more than an amount you choose. Settings › Scanner.

### Fixes
- Trades and friend requests now update straight away on your phone, and within a second or two on your friend's.

### Which file to download

- **app-release-arm64-v8a.apk**: almost every phone from the last several years.
- **app-universal-release.apk**: works on any phone. Use it if the one above won't install.
