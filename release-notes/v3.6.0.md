## Your cards in the real world

Everything from tester builds 15 to 19, in one release.

### Storage: where your cards are
- **Places** for your physical cards: shelves, boxes, binders, deck boxes and anything else, one inside another. Each copy can say where it's kept, and a card's **Where it is** lists its places, the decks using it and any copies with no place yet. Places sync with your other devices.
- **Put cards away** with the scanner: each card says where to file it, with Undo last. Give a box a sorting rule (by colour, say) and it does the thinking.
- **Sort a new pile:** scan a stack of new cards and each one gets a big pile number and where it goes — rares worth keeping, spares, cards your decks want, and bulk. Done files every pile.
- **Binder pages:** flip through a binder page by page, pocket by pocket, move or swap cards, and close the gaps. A binder can have an order, and **Fit in order** walks you through slotting new cards in, step by step.
- **Check a box:** scan everything in it and see what's missing and what doesn't belong.
- **Box labels** in three sizes, printed or saved as a PDF. Scan a label to put cards away, open the box or pull from it.
- **A copy's history** (where it's been this past year) and **Value by place**, with a spreadsheet and a PDF report.

### Building decks
- **Build this deck:** a pull list in walking order, place by place, ticked off by hand or by scanning, then moved into the deck box. **Take apart** puts every card back where it came from.
- **Primers:** an About for each deck, with [[Card]] links, shown on Stats and on shared decks.
- **Categories** of your own with targets (Ramp 10), and Group by Category with suggestions.
- **Companions**, checked against all ten conditions, **folders**, **archived decks**, and a deck's **value history**.
- **Draft and sealed:** a Limited deck with a pool, the pool by colour and its strongest pairs, and basic lands added for you.

### Collecting
- **Advanced filters** for All cards: colours and commander identity, mana value, power and toughness, legality, sets, keywords, price, artist and your copies' finish, condition and language. Each filter is a chip, the search shows as Scryfall syntax, and filters can be saved.
- **Spread thin:** the cards your decks use more copies of than you own, how many you're short and what the rest would cost, with a buy list to copy.
- **Lending cards:** lend copies to a friend or anyone by name, with a date or "next game night". Loans are grouped by person, overdue first, with Got them back and Remind. Friends on Manabind see what you've lent them under Borrowed.
- A **home-screen widget** with your collection's value and this week's change, recent price alerts, and Life counter and Scan buttons.

### At the table
- **Playgroup:** your record against each person and commander, streaks, deck ranking and your nemesis. Pick a pod to keep **one shared game log** for the whole group.
- **Game night:** who's here and what they're playing, then fair pods by power, avoiding the last game night's pairings. Start a pod and the life counter opens with everyone seated.
- **Events:** run a small Swiss or Commander pod tournament from your phone, with a round clock, standings and a **top 8 or top 4 playoff**.
- **Dungeons** (and the initiative), **rad**, **speed** and **the Ring** counters, **mulligans** on each seat, and a **life chart** and recap after every game.

### Friends
- **Messages** between friends, with an unread count.
- **Block and report**, with blocked people listed in Settings.
- **Cards for trade**, trade matches with what friends want, a trade record with ratings, and an **Activity** feed.

### Account
- **Delete my account** in Settings › Account & sync removes your account and everything synced to it. You can also ask from manabind.com/delete-account.
- A link to the **privacy policy**, at manabind.com/privacy.

### Fixes
- The life counter could crash while a panel slid in.
- A game's life chart ends where everyone ended up.

### Which file to download

- **app-release-arm64-v8a.apk**: almost every phone from the last several years.
- **app-universal-release.apk**: works on any phone. Use it if the one above won't install.
