## A big update

Everything from tester builds 10 to 14, in one release.

### Adding cards
- **One "Add to…" everywhere.** Search, card pages, binders, All cards, tag binders, the scanner and suggestions all use the same sheet: binder or deck first, then the list. Choose copies, foil and printing, put cards into the deck, its sideboard or Considering, or make a new binder or deck on the spot. A bar at the bottom says what happened, with Undo.
- **Cards are checked before they go into a deck.** A card that's banned or not legal in the deck's format, outside the commander's colours, over the copy limit (singleton in Commander and Brawl), or past the sideboard's 15 cards asks first. "Add anyway" still adds it.
- **Same words everywhere:** Import list, Export list, Move to…, Copy to…, Delete deck, Remove from deck.

### Building decks
- **Start from scratch:** pick a format, then a commander from every legal one (search, colours, most played first). Second commanders work too: Partner, Friends forever, Choose a Background and Doctor's companion. Brawl decks can be led by a legendary planeswalker.
- **Sideboard** for 60-card formats, kept with the deck and synced.
- **Playtest** with mulligans, play or draw, turns, a battlefield and your deck's tokens.
- **Only cards I own** on Suggestions, **Compare with…** another deck or an earlier version, and **Arena and MTGO export**.
- Imported lists put sideboard and maybeboard cards in the right place, not in the main deck.

### Collecting
- **Sets:** how much of each set you own, and its missing cards one tap from the Wishlist.
- **Condition and language** on your copies, kept in CSV import and export.
- **Price history** for each card, recorded on your phone from now on, and an alert when a card you own **rises** to a price you choose.
- **Breakdown** of your collection's value by set, colour, rarity and type, and your most valuable cards.
- The scanner can mark scans as foil.

### At the table
- A **Play** tab, your **deck's tokens** on your seat, a **game clock** and optional **turn timer**, and reminders for "at the beginning of your upkeep" cards.
- The game menu is grouped into Game, Table and Tools.
- Phones joined to a table can say "hold on", point at a player, keep their tokens, buzz on their turn, look up rulings, concede and roll the planar die.

### Tidier screens
- A deck's Legality is a badge at the top of Stats; Stats starts with a summary and its panels fold away.
- Card actions are grouped, deck and binder menus describe each action, and Settings is a short list of sections.

### Fixes
- Updating your binders after a trade can't move the cards twice.
- Cards added from All cards or a binder can be made commander.
- Search results always match your last search; "random card" and card pages say when they can't load, with Try again.
- Deleting a logged game asks first.

### Which file to download

- **app-release-arm64-v8a.apk**: almost every phone from the last several years.
- **app-universal-release.apk**: works on any phone. Use it if the one above won't install.
