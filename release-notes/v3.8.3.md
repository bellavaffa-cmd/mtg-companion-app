## A scanner that learns, decks that upgrade from your own cards, and collection goals

### New
- **The scanner learns from your corrections.** Fix a scan once (pick the right printing, or "It's a different card" and search for it) and the same misread comes out right next time, marked "Learned". Pick the same printing of a basic land twice and it's used whenever the set can't be read. See and forget what it learned in Settings › Scanner › Learned corrections. Synced to your other devices and manabind.com.
- **Upgrade a deck with your own cards.** A deck's Suggestions tab (or the deck menu) shows "Upgrade with my cards": swaps from cards you already own, each with the reason, where the card is, and whether the deck's bracket would go up. Swap now, Apply all checked, Consider or Not this one — and Undo. Swapped-in cards go on the deck's pull list. Tag a card keep or must keep so it's never suggested as a cut.
- **Collection goals.** Set a goal — complete a set (or one rarity of it, or in foil), playsets of a list, foil a deck, or your own list — and see its progress on the Collection home. Add what's missing to your Wishlist, see which friends have it, and get a small celebration when it's done. The scanner shows "Goal: … 41/92" when a card helps.

### Fixes
- The scanner reads the set and number in the bottom-left corner properly again: a pile of different printings of the same card (like full-art basic lands) is recognised printing by printing, and a card whose name can't be read can be recognised from its small print alone.
- One card held under the camera is counted once, instead of again and again while the scanner made up its mind about the printing.
- Starting a trade from a friend's profile could crash the app.

### Which file to download

- **app-release-arm64-v8a.apk**: almost every phone from the last several years.
- **app-universal-release.apk**: works on any phone. Use it if the one above won't install.
