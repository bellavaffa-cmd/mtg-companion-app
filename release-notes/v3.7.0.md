## Every card, everywhere

Everything from tester builds 26 to 31, in one release.

### Collection home
- **Collection now opens on a home:** your collection's value, six tiles (All cards, Storage, Binders, Sets, Sealed and graded, Loans and selling), a **To do** list and Scan, Sort a pile and Import.
- **Find anything:** type part of a name ("sol ri") to see every copy and where it is — the box and section, the deck, lent to a friend, graded — plus places and the decks using it.
- A short **what's new tour** the first time you open Collection (and again from Settings).

### Storage
- **Getting started:** set up your binders, boxes, deck boxes and shelves in three steps, print the labels, and watch the progress bar as you put cards away.
- **Upkeep:** "92% of copies have a place" and what's worth doing this week — cards with no place, boxes not checked lately, late loans, full boxes, half-done pull lists. Turn on a weekly reminder.
- **Box space:** how full each box and binder is, a warning before one overflows, and **Split into two boxes**.
- **Scan a whole binder page** in one photo: every pocket is read, with "Which one?" for printings and Record or Check against what the binder says.
- **Import with locations:** a CSV with a binder, box or location column (ManaBox, Dragon Shield, Manabind's own export) puts each card in its place.
- **Sharing storage at home:** share a shelf with someone you live with. You each keep your own cards, see each other's, and pull lists say "ask Alex" for their cards.
- **Sealed product** with where it's kept, what you paid and its value; **Open** a booster box to sort it, or a precon to make the deck.
- **Graded cards** kept apart from raw copies, with company, grade, cert and your value.
- **Gear:** sleeves (with a running-low warning), deck boxes, tokens and dice, and "This deck needs" on each deck.

### Trading and selling
- **To sell:** a list of cards to sell with quick rules, TCGplayer mass entry and a Cardmarket file, a pull list, and Mark sold.
- **Friends want these** on each binder, with Propose a trade and Bring to game night.
- **Photos of your copy:** front and back, kept on your phone, and in the Value by place report.

### Decks and wishlist
- **Deck history:** every change, named versions like "Before game night", and **Go back to this** or **Copy as new deck**.
- **Price targets on your wishlist:** tell me when a card drops under a price, with an "Under your price" box and a Buy button.
- **Pack your bag** for a game night or event: decks, tokens and counters, cards friends want and borrowed cards to give back, then Coming home.

### Account and speed
- **Backup and restore:** everything — places, loans, history and photos — in one file you keep. It works between the phone and manabind.com.
- **Data and speed** in Settings, and much faster big collections: tested with 25,000 cards.

### Fixes
- Zoomed cards stay the same size and height as you swipe through them.

### Which file to download

- **app-release-arm64-v8a.apk**: almost every phone from the last several years.
- **app-universal-release.apk**: works on any phone. Use it if the one above won't install.
