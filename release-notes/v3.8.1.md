## Start fresh when you need to

### New
- **Reset collection** in Settings › Data and speed › Danger zone. Choose what to reset:
  - **Cards only** empties every binder and the Unsorted pile, and keeps your binders, places, Wishlist and decks.
  - **Collection** also removes binders, storage places, sealed, graded, gear, loans and copy photos. Your decks stay.
  - **Everything** removes your decks as well.

  Save a backup first from the same screen, type RESET to confirm, and you have about 10 seconds to Undo. If you're signed in, the reset reaches your other devices and manabind.com, and the cards don't come back.

### Fixes
- On manabind.com, decks you'd deleted could come back after a sync that failed part-way.

### Which file to download

- **app-release-arm64-v8a.apk**: almost every phone from the last several years.
- **app-universal-release.apk**: works on any phone. Use it if the one above won't install.
