## A cleaner Collection, and cards that say where they are

### New
- **Collection, reorganised.** Four tabs — **Cards, Binders, Storage, Wishlist** — right away, under one header with your collection's value, one search bar and a "Needs attention" chip when something's waiting. The tile page is gone; Back leaves the Collection, and it opens on the tab you used last.
- **Every card says where it is.** Rows in Cards and in your binders show where the copies are — "Blue box › Instants ×2", "Talrand deck", "Lent to Sam" — and tapping one takes you there. Cards without a place say so, with a quick Put away.
- **Cards tab:** group by set, binder or place (set groups show how complete you are), and quick filters for Lent out, For sale, No place yet and Graded.
- **Binders tab:** your binders say how many places their cards are kept in, and your goals live here now.
- **Storage tab:** one Put cards away button with the rest in a menu, your places, Sealed, a new list of your graded cards, Lent out and Gear.
- **Wishlist** has its own tab. **New sets** moved to Search; Import is in the Collection menu.

### Fixes
- Cards in Virtual and Prototype decks, and in cubes, no longer count as cards you own — in Cards, the counts, set completion, "You own" and "Where you own it". Proxies never add to your collection's value.

### Which file to download

- **app-release-arm64-v8a.apk**: almost every phone from the last several years.
- **app-universal-release.apk**: works on any phone. Use it if the one above won't install.
