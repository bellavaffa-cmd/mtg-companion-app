## Sort your cards as you scan, and zoom while scanning

### New
- **Sort a pile with recipes.** Collection › Sort a pile now asks how the pile should split. Pick a ready-made recipe like Commander by colour, or make your own with up to three levels (value, colour, set, card type, rarity and more). Lay out shows numbered piles with colour bands, and Print pile signs prints the numbers big. Your recipes sync to your other devices and manabind.com. The old sorter is still there as Keep, spares, decks, bulk.
- **Hands-free scanning.** Hold a card still and it's taken without a tap; the pile shows big in its colour and the phone says it out loud ("Seven, blue"). The same card left under the camera isn't counted twice. Undo and Wrong card? fix mistakes, and a sort you leave half-way can be picked up where you stopped.
- **Smart piles.** Cards one of your decks is missing, cards a friend wants and cards a binder is missing go to their own piles first, and a copy beyond a playset is marked for trade.
- **Done, and what went where.** See each pile's count and value, cards that couldn't be read, rescan a pile to catch mistakes, offer a pile to a friend as a trade, and File everything into your collection.
- **Zoom while scanning.** Pinch, or use − 1.8× + on the scanner. Tap the zoom to go back to normal. The binder page scanner has zoom too.
- **Auto zoom and auto focus.** The scanner zooms until the card fills the outline, focuses again when the card is blurry, and sets the brightness on the card's title so foils don't come out dark. Tap the picture to focus by hand. Turn it off in Settings › Scanner › Auto zoom and focus.

### Which file to download

- **app-release-arm64-v8a.apk**: almost every phone from the last several years.
- **app-universal-release.apk**: works on any phone. Use it if the one above won't install.
