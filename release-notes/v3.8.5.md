## Sort a pile by card type

### New
- **Sort a pile › By card type.** A ready-made recipe that splits a pile into Creatures, Planeswalkers, Battles, Instants, Sorceries, Artifacts, Enchantments, Lands and Other. Cards your decks need, friends want or binders are missing still come out first. A card with two types goes with the first that fits in that order, so an artifact creature goes with Creatures. Card type is also easier to find when making your own recipe.

### Which file to download

- **app-release-arm64-v8a.apk**: almost every phone from the last several years.
- **app-universal-release.apk**: works on any phone. Use it if the one above won't install.
