## Spoiler season, cubes, trade nights, and a scanner that shows what it saw

### New
- **Last scanned card.** The scanner shows the card it just took until the next one: its set code and number (as printed bottom-left), rarity, language, foil, price, how many you own and where, and how it was recognised. Change printing, Foil and Undo are right there. If the camera reads a different printing while you're still holding the card, the panel flashes and offers to switch.
- **Search when picking the art.** Type a set name, set code, collector number or year (e.g. "fin 306") to find the right printing fast — even for basic lands with hundreds of printings.
- **Spoiler season.** Browse upcoming sets as cards are revealed, mark cards you want (they go on your Wishlist and get prices on release day), see which of your decks each card fits, and tick cards off while opening packs. Get told when newly revealed cards fit your decks.
- **Cubes.** Build a cube from your collection, see its colour and curve balance, keep it in a cube box with a pull list, share it with friends, import or export a CubeCobra list, and deal packs to draft or play sealed with your pod.
- **Trade nights.** At a game night, choose what you're bringing to trade and see what the others are bringing that you want, what they want from you, and fair trades to suggest — then tick them off at the table.
- **Goal piles.** Sort a pile can put cards your collection goals need in their own pile, and completed goals now show in your friends' Activity (turn it off in Settings › Privacy).

### Which file to download

- **app-release-arm64-v8a.apk**: almost every phone from the last several years.
- **app-universal-release.apk**: works on any phone. Use it if the one above won't install.
